Oritsemughone Ogbemi
I am a poet, essayist, and net artist with an interest in the web as a mode of transmission and in writing on multiple levels (the level of code, the level of content, and the level of poetry). I create visual essays and digital pamphlets that speak to these three levels, incorporating photocollage as a vehicle to ground my work in historical experiences of a cosmpolitan childhood and present experience of metropolitan disaster and ennui in my home city, Lagos. Thematically, I am interested in safety, lineage, romantic love, and doom.
