The purpose of this license is to be a plainly-worded guide to what you can and can't do with this work. In short, there are very few restrictions to what you can do with the material covered under this license. You are permitted to:
- copy and share the material for personal and commercial use worldwide without paying royalties.
- generate and share altered versions of the material for personal and commercial use worldwide without paying royalties.
The only thing you cannot do is redistribute or sell the unmodified collages as your own.

REDISTRIBUTE THE WORK

Please do not redistribute this work as-is under your own name. In other words, it's alright to redistribute these collages without credit, but if you have not modified them, you must not credit yourself or otherwise claim that you created them.
